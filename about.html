I am a researcher in the field of Music Information Retrieval with a background in classical music composition. My work focuses on connecting music and technology, particularly through Educational Music AI, Multimodal Music Representation, Human–AI Musical Interaction, and AI-assisted Music Creation. Currently, I am conducting research as a Ph.D. student at the Music and Audio Computing Lab (KAIST).
